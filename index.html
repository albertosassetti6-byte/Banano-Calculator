About

Everything you need to know about Banano and this calculator

What is Banano?

Banano (BAN) is a fee-less, instant, and potassium-rich cryptocurrency powered by DAG technology. It was forked from Nano in April 2018 and is distributed through folding, faucets, and community events.

How It Works

This calculator estimates your BAN earnings from Folding@home via BananoMiner. Simply enter your PPD (Points Per Day) and adjust the conversion rate if needed. The BAN price is fetched live from CoinGecko.
